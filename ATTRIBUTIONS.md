# Data Attributions

Tori bundles content from these open projects. Thank you to their maintainers.

- **Kanji data** — [davidluzgouveia/kanji-data](https://github.com/davidluzgouveia/kanji-data),
  derived from [KANJIDIC](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project)
  © Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, used under
  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- **Vocabulary lists** — [jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks](https://github.com/jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks)
  (based on Jonathan Waller's [JLPT resources](http://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/), CC-BY).
- **Stroke order diagrams** — [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/) © Ulrich Apel,
  used under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- **Grammar explanations and example sentences** — authored in this repository (grammar point
  inventory follows the commonly used public JLPT N5 syllabus lists).

An in-app attribution page (Settings → About) must ship before any public distribution.
